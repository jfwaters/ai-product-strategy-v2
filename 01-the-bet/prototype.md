# The Prototype Bet

## What I Built
A one-page MitigationIQ prototype where a planner for a fictional coastal town (Port Halden) picks a priority and gets a funded, phased flood-mitigation portfolio. It fits $20M across a federal grant, a state loan fund and a local bond, comes with an AI-written council rationale, flags the assumptions that still need verifying, and lets the planner ask "why?" and "what if?"

## Tool Used
Lovable

## Prototype Link
https://mitigation-iq.lovable.app/

## AI Value Archetype
Oracle (dominant), with a Copilot layer for "why?" and "what if?" questions.

## The Bet in One Sentence
MitigationIQ helps resilience consultants and state mitigation offices turn hazard risk, project costs and fragmented funding rules into a defensible, explainable project portfolio, because federal mitigation funding was reset in 2025–26 and communities have far more candidate projects than money.

## Kill Criteria
- **No pull from launch customers:** fewer than 3 of 10 consulting firms or state mitigation offices interviewed agree to pilot it on a real plan cycle.
- **Recommendations aren't trusted:** in pilots, planners or engineers override most of the recommended portfolio, or won't put the rationale in front of a council or funder.
- **No time saved:** it doesn't cut portfolio-building time by at least half compared with spreadsheets plus FEMA's BCA Toolkit.
- **Funding rules can't be kept current:** keeping eligibility and cost-share logic accurate across programs takes more effort than customers will pay for.
- **The data flywheel doesn't start:** customers won't record estimated, bid and final project costs, so the outcome dataset never builds.
